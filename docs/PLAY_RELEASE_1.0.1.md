## Data safety checklist

- The app collects data only when a grown-up explicitly sends a Chatty-reply report.
- Declare the applicable report content and optional note as **optional** data collection.
- Reports travel by HTTPS, so select **encrypted in transit**.
- A deletion-request method is available through the privacy-policy contact address.
- Reports are delivered to FMI, the publisher. They are not shared with a third party for advertising, analytics, or gameplay.

Use the live Play Console labels and review the resulting preview before submitting; the publisher remains responsible for the final declaration.
